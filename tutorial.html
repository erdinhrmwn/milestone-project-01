Tutorial: How to Play

This is a simple guide to help you use the football card app. It is very easy!

Step 1: Get New Cards

Go to the Reward page. There is a button called "Shuffle". Click it and wait for 5 seconds. You will see a random player card. Every card you get is saved in the list below the button.

Step 2: See Your Collection

Go to the Players page. You can see all available players there. If you like a player, click the heart icon. This will save them as your favorite. The favorite players will have a red heart.

Step 3: Admin Stuff

If you are an admin, you can login to add or delete players. New players will appear in the shuffle reward immediately.

Simple Tips:

  • Search for players by typing their name in the search box.
  • Favorites are saved in your browser (local storage).
  • Refresh the page if you want to start over.
