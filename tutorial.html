Master the Game

1

Claim Your Rewards

Head over to the Reward page. Click the "Shuffle" button to start the animation. After 5 seconds of suspense, a random player card will be drawn from our extensive database. All your rewards are tracked in your personal history!

2

Manage Your Favorites

Found a player you love? Go to the Players section. You'll see a heart icon (🤍) on each card. Click it to turn it red (❤️) and add the player to your favorites. This is saved to your browser, so they'll be there next time you visit!

3

Understand the Stats

Every card displays the player's primary position (e.g., FW for Forward, MF for Midfielder) and their nationality. Use this information to learn about the diversity of talent in the football world.

💡 Pro Tip

If you're an administrator, you can add new players to the database via the Admin Panel. These new players will immediately become available in the Reward shuffle!
