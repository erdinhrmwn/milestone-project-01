How to Play

Master the game in three simple steps. Start collecting your favorite football stars today!

Step 1: Get New Cards

Go to the Reward page. Click "Shuffle" and wait for the magic to happen! Every card you pull is saved in your history.

Step 2: See Your Collection

Visit the Players page to see all stars. Click the heart icon to save your favorites!

Step 3: Admin Management

Admins can log in to manage the player roster. New additions are instantly available for everyone to discover!

Pro Tips:

  • 01 Search for players by typing their name in the search box.
  • 02 Favorites are saved in your browser (local storage).
  • 03 Refresh the page if you want to start over.
