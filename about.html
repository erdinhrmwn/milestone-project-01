About Football Cards

The Mission

Football Cards is a project dedicated to the passion of football and the thrill of collecting. Our goal is to provide a simple yet engaging digital experience where users can discover and manage their favorite football players through a clean and intuitive interface.

How it Started

Born as a milestone project for the **Hacktiv8 Bootcamp**, this application serves as a demonstration of modern web development practices. What started as a simple player list has evolved into a dynamic platform featuring real-time rewards, persistent favorites, and a robust administrative management system.

Tech Stack

  • HTML5 & Semantic Tagging for structured and accessible content.
  • Tailwind CSS for a modern, responsive, and utility-first design approach.
  • Vanilla JavaScript for high-performance interactive logic without heavy framework overhead.
  • LocalStorage API for persistent client-side data storage, ensuring your progress is never lost.
